YOBE STATE SUSTAINABLE POWER AND IRRIGATION FOR NIGERIA (SPIN) PROJECT INAUGURATES STATE STEERING & TECHNICAL COMMITTEES

By Communication & Media Unit — Yobe SPIN Project

The Yobe State Sustainable Power and Irrigation for Nigeria (SPIN) Project has successfully inaugurated its State Steering Committee (SSC) and State Technical Committee (STC).

The inauguration marks an important milestone in strengthening project coordination, strategic direction, technical oversight, and effective implementation of SPIN Project activities in Yobe State.

The committees are expected to provide strategic guidance, facilitate inter-ministerial collaboration, address implementation challenges, and ensure that project objectives are achieved in line with the project's development goals and standards.

The inauguration further demonstrates the commitment of the Yobe State Government and relevant stakeholders towards promoting sustainable irrigation development, improving water resource management, and expanding access to reliable power for agricultural productivity and economic growth.

Together, we are committed to driving sustainable development and improving livelihoods across Yobe State.

November 9, 2026 • Jigawa State

JIGAWA SPIN PROJECT CONDUCTS SENSITIZATION MEETING WITH FARMERS, TRADITIONAL LEADERS IN WARWADE COMMUNITY

By Isma'il Ibrahim Dutse, Communication Officer — Jigawa SPIN Project

The Sustainable Power and Irrigation for Nigeria (SPIN) Project in Jigawa State has conducted a sensitization meeting with farmers and traditional leaders in Warwade community as part of its ongoing efforts to promote public awareness, strengthen community engagement, and ensure the successful implementation of the project.

The sensitization meeting, held in Warwade town, was led by the State Project Coordinator, Engr. Alhassan Abbas. It provided an important platform for constructive engagement with farmers, traditional institutions, and other community stakeholders on the objectives, benefits, planned activities, and implementation strategies of the SPIN Project.

The engagement was primarily aimed at enhancing awareness and understanding of the project among the benefiting communities, addressing concerns, correcting misconceptions, and securing the necessary support and cooperation of local stakeholders for the successful execution of the planned interventions.

Speaking during the meeting, Engr. Alhassan Abbas emphasized that effective community participation, mutual understanding, and collaboration with relevant stakeholders are essential to achieving the objectives of the SPIN Project.

He particularly addressed concerns and misinformation surrounding land ownership, categorically clarifying that the SPIN Project is not intended to seize, confiscate, or take possession of farmers' farmlands. He explained that the project works closely with local communities and relevant stakeholders to promote sustainable development while respecting indigenous land ownership and the rights of community members.

The State Project Coordinator noted that managing community expectations and ensuring that farmers receive accurate information about the project are critical components of the ongoing sensitization exercise. He stressed that the project remains committed to transparent engagement, dialogue, and cooperation with the benefiting communities throughout its implementation.

Engr. Alhassan Abbas commended farmers in the Warwade area for their hard work, dedication, and significant contributions to agricultural production and food security in Jigawa State and Nigeria at large.

He observed that the SPIN Project presents an important opportunity to enhance agricultural productivity, strengthen food security, and modernize irrigation infrastructure through sustainable irrigation practices and improved water management systems.

“We are proud of our farmers, considering their enormous contributions to food production and the development of our agricultural sector. However, with adequate sensitization and a proper understanding of the SPIN project, we believe they will appreciate its objectives and provide the necessary support and cooperation to ensure its successful implementation. We sincerely appreciate the efforts and support extended to Jigawa SPIN. This engagement has further strengthened the relationship between the project and the benefiting communities, and we look forward to sustaining this positive partnership,” he stated.

The Project Coordinator also expressed profound appreciation to the Executive Governor of Jigawa State, His Excellency, Malam Umar A. Namadi, FCA, for his continued commitment, support, and cooperation towards the successful implementation of the SPIN Project in the state since its inception.

He acknowledged that the Governor's unwavering support has continued to provide an enabling environment for the project to achieve its development objectives and deliver meaningful benefits to the people of Jigawa State.

In their separate remarks, the Village Head of Jidawa, Alhaji Baffa Shehu, and the representative of the Warwade Village Head, Buhari Musa Ado, expressed their appreciation to the Government of Jigawa State and the SPIN Project for bringing development opportunities closer to their communities.

They described the project as a welcome initiative with the potential to improve agricultural activities, enhance irrigation facilities, and strengthen the economic well-being of farmers in the area.

The traditional representatives further commended the project management for engaging directly with community members and providing clarification on issues relating to the project, particularly concerns surrounding farmland ownership.

They assured the Jigawa SPIN Project of their communities' readiness to provide all necessary support, cooperation, and assistance to facilitate smooth implementation and ensure that the project achieves its intended objectives.

The meeting also featured presentations and contributions from the Project's Monitoring and Evaluation (M&E) Officer, Malam Usaini Jafar, and Gender Officer, Hajiya Hadiza Sabo, who highlighted the importance of community participation, stakeholder cooperation, inclusiveness, and accountability in the implementation process.

They encouraged farmers, traditional leaders, and other community members to remain actively engaged with the project, cooperate with the implementation team, and utilize available engagement platforms to seek clarification, express concerns, and contribute to the successful delivery of project activities.

The officers further emphasized the importance of ensuring that women and other relevant community groups are adequately informed, actively involved, and given due consideration in project-related engagements and development interventions.

The sensitization meeting forms part of Jigawa SPIN's broader stakeholder engagement and community awareness initiatives, aimed at ensuring that benefiting communities are adequately informed, actively involved, and fully prepared for the planned interventions.

Through these engagements, the Jigawa SPIN Project seeks to build trust, promote transparency, address community concerns, and establish a strong partnership between the project implementation team and local stakeholders.

The project remains committed to working closely with the Government of Jigawa State, traditional institutions, farmers, and other relevant stakeholders to promote sustainable irrigation development, enhance agricultural productivity, strengthen food security, and contribute to the overall socio-economic development of the state.

August 30, 2026 • Katsina State

SPIN Project: Federal Team Inspects Danja Dam, Commends Katsina Govt's Commitment

By SULAIMAN AHMED MISAU, Katsina — New Nigerian Newspaper

A high powered delegation from Federal Project Monitoring Unit of the Sustainable Power and Irrigation for Nigeria (SPIN) Project were in Katsina State to inspect Danja Dam in Danja local government area of the state as part of ongoing preparations for the Project implementation.

Headed by Engineer Tawo Ogekan, the delegation arrived in Katsina at the weekend for inspection in collaboration with the State under the Project Coordinator, Engineer Shamsuddeen Sulaiman who led the two teams to the site.

The aim of the visit was to make proper assessments of the structural integrity and safety condition of the Dam ahead of proposed intervention under Sustainable Power and Irrigation for Nigeria (SPIN) Project.

Members of the Federal Project Monitoring team are: Professor Donald; Engineer M.O Offie; Engineer Usman Abubakar and Engineer Charles Okeke. Other members of the SPIN Project team from Katsina who participated in the exercise were Engineer Mustapha Dan Malam; Engineer Aminu Lawal Musa; Usman Adamu and Aisha Sifiyanu Masari.

"During the exercise, members of the Dam Safety Review Panel of Experts carried out a comprehensive assessment of the dam, inspecting both its internal and external structures to determine its current condition, identify potential safety concerns and establish the measures required to ensure that the facility remains safe, functional and fit for its intended purpose," the Katsina State Project Coordinator, Engineer Shamsudden Sulaiman said.

Engineer Sulaiman expressed his appreciation to the Federal Project Monitoring Unit and the Dam Safety Review Panel of Experts for undertaking the inspection. According to him, recommendations from the assessment would contribute tremendously to ensure safety and sustainability of the dam as well as enable People of Katsina State to derive maximum benefits from the facility.

Engineer Shamsuddeen Sulaiman commended Governor Dikko Umar Radda, for his continued commitment to improve the livelihoods of the people of the state, especially through promotion of dry-season farming and irrigation development. He also commended the State commissioner of water resources, Alhaji Mannir Ayuba Sullubawa for support and administrative counseling to ensure smooth implementation of the project in Katsina State.

Speaking, leader of the Federal delegation, Engineer Tawo Ogekan also commended Katsina State Government under the leadership of Governor Dikko Umaru Radda for its support and commitment to the implementation of the SPIN Project, noting that ensuring the structural soundness and safe operation of dams is essential to protecting communities while maximizing economic benefits.

Emir Sanusi Backs Kano SPIN's Bold Engineering Blueprint, Moves to Unlock Global Investment for Irrigation Transformation

By Maryam Mustpha Abdul — Information and Communication Specialist, SPIN Project Kano

The Emir of Kano, His Highness Khalifa Muhammadu Sanusi II, has expressed strong satisfaction with the performance, strategic direction and ambition of the Kano State Project Implementation Unit (SPIU) of the Sustainable Power and Irrigation for Nigeria (SPIN) Project.

During a two-hour high level technical and stakeholder engagement with the emir and members of the emirate council, the Kano SPIU, led by the State Project Coordinator Engr. Nuraddeen Isah Abubakar presented its proposed engineering concepts for irrigation modernization, dam rehabilitations, Kano State Integrated Water Resources Monitoring System (KS-IWRMS), alongside the Unit's broader investment and development vision for Kano State.

His Highness the former CBN governor and a global economic and financial expert commended the progress being recorded under the leadership of the State Project Coordinator, particularly the PIU's broader vision to leverage the SPIN platform to transform irrigation, agricultural productivity, sustainable power and investment in Kano State.

During the presentation, the State Project Coordinator highlighted key elements of the proposed modernization programme, including a modern open-channel irrigation system, decentralized sprinkler irrigation scheme, advanced grazing systems, modern dam instrumentation for real-time dam safety monitoring and an Irrigation Intelligence Monitoring System developed by the Kano SPIU to strengthen data-driven, informed planning and irrigation performance monitoring.

The Kano SPIU also presented its three strategic plans: the Kano SPIN Project Implementation Acceleration Plan, Kano SPIN Investment Portfolio Expansion Plan and Kano SPIN Special Project Integration and Management Plan.

His Highness pledged to support the Kano SPIN Project and lead high-level engagements, locally and internationally, aimed at mobilizing additional investment, development finance and strategic partnerships to support Kano State's ambition in transforming irrigation and agricultural economic ecosystem in the state.

The Emir further proposed the establishment of a high-level multi-stakeholder platform, which he offered to lead, bringing together relevant State and Federal institutions, development finance institutions and private-sector. Drawing from his extensive background in economics, banking, investment and risk management, His Highness further offered to provide strategic advisory support to the Kano SPIU on finance, investment and risk management, while facilitating engagements with institutions such as IFC, African Development Bank, relevant Federal MDAs, Babban Gona and other international investors.

For Kano SPIN, the vision is increasingly clear: To move beyond conventional project implementation and build an integrated, technology-driven irrigation and water resources system capable of unlocking agricultural productivity, private investment, jobs and sustainable economic growth for Kano State.

Signed: Maryam Mustpha Abdul, Information and Communication Specialist
